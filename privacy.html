Copy One Line · Privacy policy

Last updated 6 September 2026.

What the app collects

Nothing. The phone app has no account, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not connect to the internet. The browser version is covered below.

手機程式不收集任何資料。沒有帳號、沒有分析工具、沒有廣告，也不連上網路。瀏覽器版本見下文。

Your photos

If you add a photo of the line you wrote, it is stored only on your device, inside the app's own folder. It leaves the device only if you share it yourself, using your phone's share sheet. Deleting the app deletes the photos.

你加入的照片只存在這支手機、本程式自己的資料夾裡。只有你自己透過系統分享功能，照片才會離開手機。移除程式時照片也一併刪除。

The web version

If you use Copy One Line in a browser, it is served from our own server. Like any web server, it keeps a standard access log: your IP address, browser type and the time of each page load. We use this log only to count how many people open the app each day. Raw log lines are deleted after ten days; only daily totals are kept, with addresses replaced by a one-way hash. Your writing, photos and settings stay in your browser and are never sent to the server. No cookies, no third-party analytics, no advertising.

若你在瀏覽器中使用本程式，它由我們自己的伺服器提供。伺服器只保留一般的存取記錄（IP 位址、瀏覽器種類、載入時間），用來計算每天有多少人打開程式。原始記錄十天後刪除，只保留每日總數，位址以單向雜湊取代。你的照片與設定只存在你的瀏覽器裡，不會傳到伺服器。沒有 cookie、沒有第三方分析工具、沒有廣告。

Reminders

If you choose a reminder time, the app schedules one local notification a day on your device. No information is sent anywhere.

Permissions

Camera and photo library: only to keep a photo of the line you wrote. Notifications: only if you choose a reminder time.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/wwwlqh/copy-one-line-prototype.
